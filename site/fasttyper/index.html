FastTyper

A local-LLM-based spell and grammar checker that rapidly corrects text in real-time, as you type. It uses a fine-tuned model (not made by me) that runs on your GPU and only changes typos, never modifying the original wording or meaning. I designed it to speed up my note-taking in lectures, allowing me to focus on the content without constantly back-tracking to fix typos.

Note: FastTyper is currently a work in progress, with support only for Linux and AMD GPUs at the moment.
